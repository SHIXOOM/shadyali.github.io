Research

Three threads at Minnesota NLP, all about what a model knows and how we measure it.

  • Concepts inside item response models

    An additive item response model built on activation-derived semantic concepts. It improved response prediction and recovered MMLU-Pro model rankings using fewer questions than standard IRT or random selection, and traces predicted shifts in correctness to concepts and the tokens that activate them.

    A Concept-Informed Item Response Model, in preparation

  • Model internals for legal experts

    With Thomson Reuters, I proposed inspecting smaller surrogate models to bring activation descriptions into expert annotation of legal reasoning. Across three models, surrogacy was stronger within model families than across them. I coordinated the researchers and legal experts.

    Bringing Model Internals, under review at ICLR 2027

  • Who you test matters

    How the set of evaluated models changes conclusions about benchmark quality. I proposed the Capability Alignment Score (CAS), a Classical Test Theory-based information metric that was more stable with small model populations and selected smaller subsets that better preserved rankings.

    Who You Test Matters, EvalEval at ACL 2026
